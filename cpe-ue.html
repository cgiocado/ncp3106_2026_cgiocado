The program

Computer Engineering at UE

A closer look at the BS Computer Engineering program: what it aims to teach, how it's structured, and what students walk away with.

Overview

A hardware-first foundation, built for real systems

The BS Computer Engineering program at the University of the East trains students to design, build, and troubleshoot the systems where electronics and software meet. The curriculum moves from core mathematics and circuits in the early years toward embedded systems, networks, and a culminating capstone project in the later years.

Program objectives

  • Apply engineering fundamentals to real hardware/software problems
  • Design, test, and document computer-based systems
  • Work effectively within technical and interdisciplinary teams
  • Practice ethical, standards-compliant engineering

Skills developed

  • Circuit design and digital logic
  • Embedded programming (C/C++, assembly)
  • Computer networks and systems administration
  • Problem-solving through project-based learning

Learning experience

  • Lecture paired with hands-on laboratory work
  • Group design projects each academic year
  • Industry immersion and on-the-job training
  • A senior capstone project from concept to prototype
Where the work happens

Laboratories & facilities

Coursework is reinforced in dedicated laboratories where students build and test what they've learned in lecture.

Digital logic lab

Breadboards, logic gates, and simulation tools for foundational circuit design.

Microprocessor lab

Microcontroller kits for embedded programming and hardware interfacing.

Networking lab

Routers, switches, and structured cabling for hands-on networking exercises.

Computer & research lab

Workstations for software development, simulation, and capstone research.

Beyond the classroom

Student projects & research

Students apply what they've learned through design projects, research activities, and academic opportunities such as paper presentations and engineering competitions.

Design projects

Course-embedded builds, from simple circuits to multi-part embedded systems.

Capstone research

A year-long senior project addressing a real engineering problem.

Academic opportunities

Competitions, conferences, and industry-linked training programs.
