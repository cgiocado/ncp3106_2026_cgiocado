Where a CpE degree can take you

Computer Engineering graduates aren't limited to one job title. Here are fifteen common paths — and what each one actually involves.

Computer Engineer

Designs and maintains the hardware/software systems inside electronic devices.

Embedded Systems Engineer

Develops firmware and hardware for purpose-built devices like appliances and automotive controllers.

IoT Engineer

Builds connected devices and the systems that let them communicate and share data.

Software Engineer

Designs, writes, and tests applications and systems software.

Hardware Engineer

Designs and tests physical computing components such as circuit boards and processors.

Network Engineer

Plans, builds, and maintains the networks that connect devices and organizations.

Systems Engineer

Oversees how hardware, software, and processes work together as one system.

Cybersecurity Engineer

Protects networks, systems, and data from unauthorized access and attack.

Data Engineer

Builds the pipelines and infrastructure that move and store data reliably.

AI / Machine Learning Engineer

Designs and trains models that let systems learn from data.

Cloud Engineer

Builds and manages applications and infrastructure hosted on cloud platforms.

DevOps Engineer

Automates and streamlines how software is built, tested, and deployed.

Robotics Engineer

Designs robots and the control systems that let them sense and act.

Technical Support Engineer

Diagnoses and resolves hardware and software issues for end users.

Research & Development Engineer

Explores new technologies and prototypes future engineering solutions.
