About SCPES

Objectives

SCPES exists to strengthen the CpE community at UE by giving students a space to build technical skills, take on leadership roles, and connect with peers who share the same interests.

What SCPES does

  • Organizes technical workshops and seminars
  • Runs internal and inter-school competitions
  • Supports student research and design projects
  • Represents CpE students in college-wide activities

Getting involved

  • Open to all enrolled CpE students
  • Committee roles in events, technical, and creatives teams
  • Officer elections held each academic year
  • No prior experience required to join

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about CpE

What's the difference between Computer Engineering and Computer Science?
Computer Science focuses mainly on software, algorithms, and theory. Computer Engineering covers that plus the hardware side — circuits, processors, and embedded systems — and how hardware and software work together.
Is Computer Engineering mostly hardware?
No. It's a balance of both. Students study digital circuits and hardware design alongside programming, networking, and software development.
Do Computer Engineering students learn programming?
Yes. Programming is a core part of the curriculum, especially for embedded systems, where software runs directly on hardware.
What programming languages are commonly used?
C and C++ are common for embedded and systems-level work, alongside Python for scripting, data analysis, and machine learning coursework.
What careers can CpE graduates pursue?
Roles spanning hardware, software, networks, and security — see the full list on the Careers page.
What skills should incoming CpE students develop?
A solid grounding in math and physics helps, along with curiosity about how devices work and willingness to learn through hands-on building and debugging.
