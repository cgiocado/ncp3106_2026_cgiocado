What is Computer Engineering?

Computer Engineering sits at the intersection of electronics and computer science. It's the discipline behind the chips, circuit boards, and embedded software that let physical devices sense, compute, and act — from a traffic light controller to a self-driving car.

Read more

Why study CpE at UE?

The BS Computer Engineering program at the University of the East pairs a hardware-first foundation with hands-on labs, faculty mentorship, and an active student organization — preparing graduates for both engineering practice and further research.
